Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Sharada Sarees, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with us via our website, WhatsApp, or social media.

1. Information We Collect

To process your orders and provide live support, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address, and shipping/billing address.
  • Order Information: Product selections, payment preferences (Prepaid/COD), and transaction IDs.
  • Communication Data: Chat histories, call logs, or video call metadata via WhatsApp for customer support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, dispatch, and deliver your saree orders.
  • To communicate with you regarding order status, live video shopping, and customer support.
  • To improve our manufacturing, product offerings, and website experience.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations in India.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized Sharada Sarees operational staff.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your name, phone number, and address with trusted courier partners (like Delhivery, India Post, etc.) strictly for the purpose of delivering your order.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at sharadasarees.operations@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
