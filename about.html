About Sharada Sarees

Our Story

Founded in 2015 in Belagavi, Karnataka, Sharada Sarees is a saree manufacturing house with 10+ years of hands-on experience in weaving and producing silk, semi-silk and polyester sarees. What began at the loom has today grown into a family of beautiful designs loved across the region.

Manufacturer → Direct to Customer

We have recently started our D2C (Direct-to-Customer) journey. By selling directly from our factory, we remove middlemen and bring you authentic quality at factory-direct prices. Our own branded website & domain are launching soon — until then, we're one WhatsApp message away.

How We Serve You

Customers reach us through our social media pages and connect with us on WhatsApp. You can discuss products over live chat, calls and even video calls to see the real saree before ordering. Orders are accepted as prepaid or Cash on Delivery, and our support continues even after delivery.
