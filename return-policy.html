Return, Refund & Replacement Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. 7-Day Return & Refund Policy

At Sharada Sarees, your satisfaction is our priority. If you are not completely happy with your purchase, you may request a return and refund within 7 days of the delivery date. To be eligible, the saree must be:

  • Unused, unwashed, and unstitched.
  • In its original condition with all tags and packaging intact.
  • Accompanied by the original invoice or order confirmation.

Once the returned item is received and inspected at our Belagavi facility, we will notify you of the approval. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

2. 15-Day Replacement Policy

If your saree arrives with a manufacturing defect (e.g., torn fabric, missing zari, major weaving flaws) or was damaged during transit, we offer a free replacement within 15 days of delivery.

Please send clear photos or a short video of the defect to our WhatsApp support team before initiating a return claim so we can resolve it quickly.

3. How to Initiate a Return or Replacement

  • WhatsApp (Fastest): Message us at +91 97397 53101 with your order details and photos.
  • Email: Send details to sharadasarees.operations@gmail.com.
  • Our live support team will guide you through the return pickup or replacement process.

4. Non-Returnable Cases

  • Products that have been worn, washed, altered, or smelled of perfume/smoke.
  • Minor colour variations due to different mobile/monitor screen settings (this is not considered a defect).
  • Customized or specially ordered pieces, unless they have a manufacturing defect.
💬 Need help? Our team is available on WhatsApp for live chat, calls, or video calls to assist you with any post-purchase queries.
